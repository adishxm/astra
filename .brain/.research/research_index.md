## Evidence hierarchy used for decisions

1. Project-specific SIH report and ECDAT-X master dossier define product goals but contain reconstructed/aspirational elements; exact official SIH statement and administrative constraints remain a confirmation item.
2. Curated academic papers and standards mapping justify methods and cautions; standards/guidance are not interchangeable with peer-reviewed evidence.
3. Competitive dossier is positioning input, not independent competitor benchmarking.
4. Unrelated ePrint papers are explicitly retained and mapped as context or excluded; their presence is not treated as a feature request.
5. No claim of actual detection accuracy, model performance, vendor comparison, or delivery outcome is made by this planning package.
